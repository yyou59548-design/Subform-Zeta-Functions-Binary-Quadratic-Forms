# Subform Zeta Functions of Quadratic Lattices

This repository contains two related research manuscripts.

## Higher-dimensional extension — new preprint (2026)

**[Subform Zeta Functions of Integral Quadratic Lattices in Arbitrary Rank](higher-rank/README.md)** — Zhanhe Zhang.

**Main claim:** the manuscript resolves the positive-definite arbitrary-rank extension problem proposed by D. Kim, S. H. Lee, and S. Lee in *Mathematische Annalen* (2025). It gives exact all-index intrinsic-isometry class counts, general analytic results, and a parity criterion for GL/SL equivalence; further theorems treat indefinite rank at least three.

- [Full 48-page manuscript (PDF)](higher-rank/Zhang_Subform_Zeta_Arbitrary_Rank_2026.pdf)
- [Complete LaTeX source archive](higher-rank/arxiv_source_48pp.zip)
- [Detailed theorem statements and research scope](higher-rank/README.md)

**Status:** publicly available author preprint; not yet peer-reviewed. This is a precise claim about the results presented in the manuscript, not a claim that every extension or natural-boundary problem is settled.

## Earlier binary-conductor work

**Subform Zeta Functions of Positive Definite Binary Quadratic Forms of Arbitrary Conductor** — Zhanhe Zhang.

This earlier project studies positive definite binary quadratic forms with arbitrary conductor and supplies the background for the higher-rank study. Its original manuscript remains in the repository.
