# Subform Zeta Functions of Integral Quadratic Lattices in Arbitrary Rank

**Research preprint — Z. Zhang (2026)**

This project presents a solution to the **positive-definite arbitrary-rank extension problem** posed by **D. Kim, S. H. Lee, and S. Lee** in *Zeta Functions Enumerating Subforms of Quadratic Forms*, *Mathematische Annalen* **393** (2025), 1459–1514. It studies the intrinsic integral-isometry classes represented by finite-index sublattices of a fixed quadratic lattice.

The manuscript extends the earlier binary theory to **all positive-definite integral quadratic lattices of rank `n >= 2`** and supplies further results in indefinite signature. These statements describe the author's research results; the preprint has not yet been independently peer-reviewed.

## Main results

1. **Higher-dimensional counting:** exact all-index coefficient formulas for both ordinary (`GL`) and proper (`SL`) intrinsic isometry, using canonical restoration and a signed parent-incidence formula.
2. **Analytic theory in positive rank:** for each positive-definite rank-`n` parent, meromorphic continuation to `Re(s) > n - 1`, with a simple pole at `s = n`, and the asymptotic
   `sum_{m <= X} a_H(m) = (R_H/n) X^n + O_{L,epsilon}(X^(n-1+epsilon))`.
3. **Parity criterion:** the ordinary and proper coefficient sequences agree for every index **if and only if the rank is odd**, in every nondegenerate signature.
4. **Indefinite rank at least three:** formulas via represented spinor classes and a two-term asymptotic featuring a strictly positive `X (log X)^(3/2)` secondary term.
5. **Explicit examples:** all-index formulas for cubic ternary lattices, quaternary orientation phenomena, and further results on Euler products and analytic singularities.

The work also addresses the higher-rank analytic assertions associated with **Conjecture 7.2** of the authors' [2024 preprint, version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05625), identifying a necessary **odd-rank parity correction** for the determinant-negative contribution.

## Scope

The principal positive-definite higher-rank generalization is treated. The manuscript does **not** claim to settle every possible analytic-continuation or natural-boundary problem, or the exact growth exponent for all nonsplit indefinite binary lattices.
